# Cozy Farming Simulator MVP: help

> Owner: before you publish this page, put your support address under "Still stuck?". The game prints it too if you build with `VITE_SUPPORT=you@example.com npm run pack`.

## Where is my farm?

In the browser you play in, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. The game saves every few seconds, at each door you walk through, and each night.

That also means a farm does not follow you. A different browser, a different device or a private window starts a new one.

## Keep it safe

Open the menu, go to **Options**, and choose **Back up your farm**. It saves one file. Keep the file somewhere safe, or use it to move your farm to another browser or device with **Restore a backup**. A restore keeps the farm it replaces until you have slept a night in the new one, and **Undo restore** brings it back.

Do this before you:

- clear your browser's site data or history
- switch browsers or devices
- leave a phone browser alone for a long while. Safari can clear a site's saved data when you have not visited for a while. Adding the game to your Home Screen may help, and a backup file always does. Nobody has tried this on a real iPhone yet.

## The game will not start

- It needs a browser with WebGL. If yours does not have it, the game says so on screen. Try another browser, or switch on hardware acceleration in your browser's settings.
- Some private windows block saving. The title warns you before you play if this browser will not keep your farm.
- Reload the page. Your farm is saved.

## Two tabs

Only one tab can play a farm. If you open a second, it asks first, and the first one stops playing so they cannot overwrite each other.

## Sound

The game stays quiet until you tap or press a key. Music and Sounds each have a volume in **Options**, and the title has a Sound button.

## Which browsers have been tried

- Chromium on a computer and in phone-sized windows: played through, with automatic checks on every screen size from 280 to 3440 pixels wide.
- WebKit (Safari's engine) with no screen: drew the title, started a farm, saved, and offered Continue after a reload.
- Firefox with no screen: loaded with no errors.
- A real iPhone or Android phone: not yet.

## What the game keeps to itself

Nothing leaves your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics and no requests once the game has loaded. The only copy of your farm besides the browser's storage is a backup file you chose to save.
